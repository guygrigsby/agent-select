# Google Models Research

## Corrected scope

The assigned 19 Google models were checked against saved repository sources. Numeric coverage is model-specific: only Gemma 4 26B A4B IT and Gemma 4 31B IT have saved benchmark evidence. The 26B comparison-table values are mapped to `data/research/google/sources/hf-gemma-4-26b.html` and explicitly to its Gemma 4 26B A4B column; they are not attributed to the 31B model. Gemini catalog/blog claims remain claims, not benchmark evidence. Future/preview models remain unresearched. Source rights are unknown unless stated by the artifact.

## Evidence and limitations

Gemma scores are provider-reported from saved Hugging Face model-card material; no independent verification was captured. The attempted Pro blog artifact is recorded as redirect/not-found rather than retrieved content; the March 2025 artifact is retained as the usable blog source. Missing benchmark evidence is explicit and no neighboring model's score is inherited.
