# Temporal model identity

Proposed contract. Owns offer/alias revision bindings, their later ending facts and identity-policy records. No display-name matching or newest-row selection proves identity. Historical measurements retain their original revision evidence.

## Facts, ownership and time

ProviderOffer owns immutable Binding, BindingEnd and BindingSupersession value facts. A source-scoped executable alias is the same offer identified by `(provider,upstream_id)`, not a second temporal aggregate. Source-only aliases may be represented as non-executable offers; catalog presence never grants host eligibility. ModelRevision is referenced by ID, never renamed through an alias. No independent alias-establishment/events table or competing alias-currentness algorithm exists.

Binding fields: `id`, `offer_id`, `revision_id`, `evidence_assertion_id`, `authority_policy_version`, `freshness_policy_version`, `effective_at` and `recorded_at`. `recorded_at` is assigned by the collector's durable commit, not trusted caller input. `effective_at` is the evidenced publisher effective time; if the source establishes only a present observation, use the observation time and do not backdate it. Evidence has to identify the exact immutable date/variant/configuration. Constructor rejects unsupported identities and policy versions unknown at record time.

BindingEnd fields: `id`, `binding_id`, `kind: close|retract`, `evidence_assertion_id`, both policy versions, `effective_at` and `recorded_at`. Close ends support from its effective time. Retraction withdraws the specific claim from its evidenced correction time, which may precede establishment. A retraction cannot be cancelled by rediscovering the same evidence; a genuinely corrected claim needs new evidence and a new binding.

BindingSupersession fields: `id`, `old_binding_id`, `replacement_binding_id`, `evidence_assertion_id`, both policy versions, `effective_at` and `recorded_at`. It ends the old binding only when the source explicitly supports that replacement. A newly observed destination alone cannot close the old claim. Supersession requires same offer, distinct binding IDs, acyclic references and a replacement effective no later than the supersession. Each fact remains meaningful without nullable replacement/closure fields.

Authority and freshness are immutable, version-addressed policy inputs. Authority policy enumerates allowed source/publisher/claim-type triples. Freshness policy enumerates source/claim-type maximum age. No match, policy conflict, expired confirmation or unavailable version is unknown, never implicit permission. Policies restrict which claims are supported; they do not choose the newest among conflicting supported revisions. A publisher reconciliation must explicitly end the losing claims.

## Deterministic resolution

Inputs are offer ID, effective-time instant `e`, knowledge cutoff `t` and both policy versions. Ordinary present routing uses the snapshot's pinned knowledge cutoff and the current effective time; historical replay pins both. Use only policy/evidence/fact records committed by `t`. Late arrivals cannot alter an earlier pinned replay.

1. Enumerate establishments recorded by `t` and effective by `e` whose identity evidence is valid and supported under the pinned authority policy. Select the latest successful confirmation with confirmed_at<=e and recorded_at<=t, then check its age against the pinned finite freshness ceiling at `e`. A confirmation must be known by `t`; repeating identical bytes confirms the same claim without creating a new revision.
2. Remove any establishment with a known close/retract effective by `e`. Remove it for a supersession only if the same-offer replacement, evidence and policy checks pass. Malformed or contradictory transition evidence is an identity conflict, not ignored evidence permitting the old claim.
3. Collect distinct remaining revision IDs. None means unknown. One means resolved, including multiple corroborating claims of that same revision. More than one means ambiguous. An unresolved equal-time replacement conflict means ambiguous even if filtering a stale candidate would otherwise leave one revision; reconciliation must explicitly establish which transition is valid.
4. Return resolved revision or unknown/ambiguous, supporting and ending fact IDs, `e`, `t` and policy versions. Never tie-break by observation time, source arrival or ID.

Retraction/closure facts are historical evidence; their effect does not expire merely because their citation ages. Rechecking freshness cannot resurrect ended bindings. Historical measurement identity is resolved against its evidenced evaluation revision/time and pinned knowledge cutoff, not by the current alias destination. If evaluation time or exact revision identity is unknown, preserve the raw score with unresolved identity.

## Relational contract

All columns are NOT NULL. BYTEA hashes require length 32. FK deletion is restrictive. ProviderOffer owns binding/end/supersession rows; policy rows are immutable configuration value records referenced by snapshots and binding facts, not a new mutable aggregate.

- `identity_authority_policies`: `version TEXT PK`, `content_hash BYTEA UNIQUE`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`. Index PK serves pinned-policy lookup.
- `identity_authority_rules`: `policy_version TEXT FK identity_authority_policies`, `source_id TEXT FK sources`, `publisher TEXT`, `claim_type TEXT CHECK IN ('revision_binding','binding_end','binding_supersession')`; PK `(policy_version,source_id,publisher,claim_type)`. Absence denies. PK serves allowed-source lookup.
- `identity_freshness_policies`: `version TEXT PK`, `content_hash BYTEA UNIQUE`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`. PK serves pinned-policy lookup.
- `identity_freshness_rules`: `policy_version TEXT FK identity_freshness_policies`, `source_id TEXT FK sources`, `claim_type TEXT CHECK IN ('revision_binding')`, `maximum_age_seconds BIGINT CHECK >0`; PK `(policy_version,source_id,claim_type)`. PK serves confirmation-age admission.
- `identity_bindings`: `id TEXT PK`, `offer_id TEXT FK offers`, `revision_id TEXT FK model_revisions`, `evidence_assertion_id TEXT FK assertions`, `authority_policy_version TEXT FK identity_authority_policies`, `freshness_policy_version TEXT FK identity_freshness_policies`, `effective_at TIMESTAMPTZ`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`; UNIQUE `(offer_id,revision_id,evidence_assertion_id,authority_policy_version,freshness_policy_version,effective_at)`. Index `(offer_id,effective_at,recorded_at)` serves temporal candidate lookup; `(revision_id)` serves historical provenance.
- `identity_binding_confirmations`: `binding_id TEXT FK identity_bindings`, `observation_id TEXT FK observations`, `confirmed_at TIMESTAMPTZ`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`; PK `(binding_id,observation_id)`. Index `(binding_id,confirmed_at DESC,recorded_at)` serves freshness at pinned knowledge cutoff. Validation requires the observation to confirm this exact claim and `confirmed_at` to be its successful observation time. Establishment includes its first confirmation in the same ProviderOffer transaction.
- `identity_binding_ends`: `id TEXT PK`, `binding_id TEXT FK identity_bindings`, `kind TEXT CHECK IN ('close','retract')`, `evidence_assertion_id TEXT FK assertions`, `authority_policy_version TEXT FK identity_authority_policies`, `freshness_policy_version TEXT FK identity_freshness_policies`, `effective_at TIMESTAMPTZ`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`; UNIQUE `(binding_id,kind,evidence_assertion_id,effective_at)`. Index `(binding_id,effective_at,recorded_at)` serves ending-fact anti-join.
- `binding_supersessions`: `id TEXT PK`, `old_binding_id TEXT FK identity_bindings`, `replacement_binding_id TEXT FK identity_bindings`, `evidence_assertion_id TEXT FK assertions`, `authority_policy_version TEXT FK identity_authority_policies`, `freshness_policy_version TEXT FK identity_freshness_policies`, `effective_at TIMESTAMPTZ`, `recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ`; CHECK old!=replacement; UNIQUE `(old_binding_id,replacement_binding_id,evidence_assertion_id,effective_at)`. Index `(old_binding_id,effective_at,recorded_at)` serves supersession anti-join; `(replacement_binding_id)` serves chain validation.

Deferred constraints and publication validation enforce same-offer references, replacement effective-time order, evidence-policy authorization and acyclic chains. Competing facts may coexist so ambiguity remains observable. Immutable policy content hashes are validated against their ordered rules. No `valid_until` is stored on a live binding and no latest-observation index defines currentness.

## Service and events

All mutations use Collector worker/Coordinator service identity with evidence-validator source assignment, existing lease fencing where invoked from collection and no operator-supplied recorded timestamps. Operator alone registers immutable policies. Publisher may read but not invent bindings. All ports return fact ID and recorded time, use the existing closed errors (`invalid_request`, `forbidden`, `invalid_evidence`, `identity_unresolved`, `conflict`, `stale_lease`) and are idempotent by their semantic fact/evidence fingerprint; same fingerprint with different bytes conflicts.

- `register_identity_policy(kind,version,ordered_rules,content_hash)`: Operator, configuration-write authority; persists the named immutable policy values. No identity-domain aggregate transition/event is invented. A durable internal configuration-change notification IdentityPolicyAvailable carries kind/version/content hash/recorded time; SourceAdmission and EvidenceProjection invalidate dependent jobs/snapshots under IdentityResolution. At-least-once delivery and version/hash deduplication apply. This is infrastructure notification, not SourcePolicyChanged emitted by an unrelated aggregate.
- `bind_offer(offer_id,revision_id,evidence_id,effective_at,authority_version,freshness_version)`: evidence validator invokes ProviderOffer.EstablishBinding through IdentityResolution, then emits IdentityBindingEstablished. Response binding ID and collector-assigned recorded time.
- `confirm_binding(binding_id,observation_id)`: evidence validator invokes ProviderOffer.ConfirmBinding and emits IdentityBindingConfirmed. Same claim only; response confirmation fact ID/recorded time.
- `end_binding(binding_id,kind,evidence_id,effective_at,authority_version,freshness_version)`: evidence validator invokes ProviderOffer.EndBinding and emits IdentityBindingEnded.
- `supersede_binding(old_id,replacement_id,evidence_id,effective_at,authority_version,freshness_version)`: evidence validator invokes ProviderOffer.SupersedeBinding and emits IdentityBindingSuperseded. IdentityResolution owns cross-reference/evidence rules; this mutates one offer aggregate only.
- `resolve_identity(offer_id,e,t,authority_version,freshness_version)`: Host adapter or snapshot Publisher, read-only local authority; invokes IdentityResolution.Resolve and returns the complete resolution above. Pure/idempotent for pinned inputs; no domain event or new table.

Each binding event is internal, emitted by ProviderOffer on its named fact transition, with offer/binding/fact IDs, evidence/observation IDs, effective and recorded times and policy versions; supersession adds old/replacement IDs, ending adds kind. Transactional outbox gives at-least-once delivery and per-offer order. EvidenceProjection consumes and recomputes successor identity inputs under IdentityResolution; consumers deduplicate event ID. Snapshot identity entries retain status, both cutoffs, policy versions and evidence IDs. Ambiguous/unknown identity cannot supply revision-derived quality or traits.

## Verification cases

Resolve A, supersede to B with explicit evidence, then retain A's benchmark on A. Two supported live A/B claims are ambiguous. Same-revision corroboration remains resolved. Two equal-time conflicting replacements remain ambiguous. Late retraction changes present knowledge of the past but not a replay pinned before its arrival. Expired confirmation yields unknown, never a predecessor guess. Ended bindings do not return when ending evidence ages. Reject cross-offer or cyclic supersession. Each case pins `e`, `t` and immutable policies.
